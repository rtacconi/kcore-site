Questions

Can I use the free version in production?

Yes. The Community edition is the full kcore hypervisor running on a single node. There are no artificial limits on the number of VMs or resources you can use.

What counts as clustering?

Running multiple kcore nodes managed by a single controller with automatic scheduling, failover, and live migration between nodes.

Is kcore open source?

The core hypervisor is open source on GitHub. Enterprise clustering and advanced features are available under a commercial licence.
