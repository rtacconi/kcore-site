FAQ

Is kcore open source?

Yes. The Community Edition core is Apache-2.0. Community ISOs are on GitHub Releases.

Do I need a subscription to try kcore?

No. Download the Community ISO. Subscriptions are for the official signed distribution, enterprise update channel, and commercial support.

What does a subscription include?

Official signed ISO images, stable enterprise updates, tested production releases, and support per tier. Optional offline subscription key activation may be offered when available.

How is pricing calculated?

Per physical CPU socket, not per core. Enterprise has an 8-socket minimum; ask for multi-year or fleet quotes.

How do I pay?

Use Buy on a tier. If a Stripe Payment Link is configured it opens checkout; otherwise email team@tacconiconsulting.com and we will send an invoice or Stripe link.
